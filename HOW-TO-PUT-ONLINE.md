# How to put your Pixel Craft site online

## Option A: Cloudflare Pages (recommended)

1. **Sign up** at [Cloudflare](https://dash.cloudflare.com/sign-up) (free).
2. Go to **Pages** in the left menu → **Create a project** → **Direct Upload**.
3. Click **Upload assets** and drag **this whole folder** (`digital-store`) or all the files inside it (`index.html`, `styles.css`, `script.js`) into the upload area.
4. **Project name**: e.g. `pixel-craft` (this will be your URL: `pixel-craft.pages.dev`).
5. Click **Deploy**. In 1–2 minutes your site will be live at `https://pixel-craft.pages.dev` (or whatever name you chose).

---

## Option B: GitHub Pages (no drag-and-drop, but free)

1. **Sign up** at [GitHub](https://github.com/signup) (free).
2. Click **New repository** → name it e.g. `pixel-craft` → **Create repository**.
3. On the new repo page, click **“uploading an existing file”**.
4. Drag and drop `index.html`, `styles.css`, and `script.js` from this folder, then **Commit changes**.
5. Go to **Settings** → **Pages** (left sidebar).
6. Under **Source**, choose **Deploy from a branch**.
7. Branch: **main**, folder: **/ (root)** → **Save**.
8. Wait 1–2 minutes. Your site will be at: `https://YOUR_GITHUB_USERNAME.github.io/pixel-craft/`

---

Your Formspree form will work on both — contact and “Book a call” will still send to your email.
